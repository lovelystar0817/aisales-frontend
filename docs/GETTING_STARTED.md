# Getting Started

This guide will help you get up and running with the AI Voice Training Platform.

## Prerequisites

*   A modern web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari).
*   A microphone (built-in or external headset) for roleplay features.
*   An account invitation from your administrator.

## Accessing the Application

1.  Navigate to the application URL provided by your organization (e.g., `https://trainapi.hupo.co` or your custom domain).
2.  You will be redirected to the **Auth0** login page.

## Authentication

The platform uses **Auth0** for secure, enterprise-grade authentication.

### Logging In

1.  Enter your organizational email address.
2.  Enter your password.
3.  If your organization uses Single Sign-On (SSO), select "Log in with [Your Company]" if available.

### Guest Access

Some deployments support guest access for quick demos:

1.  Navigate to `/guest/auth`.
2.  Follow the prompts to access a limited set of training scenarios.

## First-Time Setup

Upon your first login:

1.  **Microphone Permission:** The browser will ask for permission to access your microphone. You **must allow** this for the roleplay features to function.
2.  **Language Selection:** You may be asked to select your preferred training language (English, Indonesian, or Malay).

## Navigation Bar

Once logged in, you will see the main navigation bar:

*   **Home:** Your dashboard with recent progress.
*   **Practices:** Access past roleplay sessions.
*   **Call Analysis:** Upload audio files for review.
*   **Profile/Logout:** Manage your account settings.

# Management Portal

The **Management Portal** (located at `/manage`) is designed for administrators, HR managers, and team leads to oversee training programs.

## Accessing the Portal

1.  Log in with an account that has **Admin** privileges.
2.  Click the **Manage** link in the navigation bar (or go directly to `/manage`).

## Dashboard Overview

The Admin Dashboard provides high-level metrics:

*   **Total Users:** Number of active trainees.
*   **Sessions Completed:** Total roleplays conducted across the organization.
*   **Average Score:** The organization-wide performance metric.
*   **Usage Trends:** Graphs showing platform adoption over time.

## Key Sections

*   **Users:** Manage employee accounts and permissions.
*   **Settings:** Configure global platform settings.
*   **Products:** Import and manage product knowledge bases that the AI uses during roleplays.
*   **Dashboard:** View aggregate analytics.

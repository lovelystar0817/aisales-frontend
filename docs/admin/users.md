# User Management

As an administrator, you can manage the users who have access to the platform.

## Viewing Users

Navigate to **Users** in the sidebar to see a paginated list of all registered users.

*   **Search:** Find users by name or email.
*   **Filter:** Filter by role (Admin, User) or status.

## User Details

Click on a specific user to view their detailed profile:

*   **Activity Log:** See when they last logged in.
*   **Performance History:** Review their session scores and progress.
*   **Transcript Access:** (If enabled by policy) Review the specific transcripts of their training sessions for coaching purposes.

## Managing Roles

You can assign different roles to users:

*   **User:** Standard access to training modules.
*   **Admin:** Full access to the Management Portal.

## Bulk Operations

*   **Wipe Usage:** Reset usage stats for testing purposes (available via API/Scripts).
*   **Import:** (If configured) Bulk import users via CSV or sync with your HRIS.

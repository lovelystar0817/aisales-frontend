# User Dashboard

The **User Dashboard** is your command center for tracking progress and accessing training modules.

## Home Screen

*   **Recent Activity:** Shows your most recent roleplay sessions and their scores.
*   **Recommended Modules:** Suggests new scenarios based on your past performance.
*   **Progress Charts:** Visualizes your improvement over time.

## Practices / Past Sessions

Navigate to `/practices/past` to see a history of all your sessions.

*   **Filter:** Sort by date, scenario type, or score.
*   **Review:** Click on any past session to re-read the transcript and review the AI's feedback.

## Profile Settings

Access your profile to:

*   Change your display language.
*   View your account details.
*   Log out securely.

# AI Roleplay

The **AI Roleplay** feature is the core of the training platform. It allows you to practice sales pitches, objection handling, and customer support scenarios with an AI agent that reacts in real-time.

## Starting a Session

1.  From the **Home** dashboard, select a training module or scenario (e.g., "Sales Pitch," "Objection Handling").
2.  Click **Start Session**.
3.  The application will connect to the **LiveKit** server to establish a low-latency audio connection.

## The Interface

The roleplay interface consists of:

*   **AI Avatar:** A visual representation of the customer you are talking to.
*   **Microphone Controls:** Mute/unmute your microphone.
*   **End Session Button:** Stop the roleplay at any time.
*   **Real-time Transcript:** (Optional) See text captions of the conversation as it happens.

## During the Session

1.  **Speak Naturally:** Talk to the AI just as you would a real customer.
2.  **Listen:** The AI will respond based on your input, tone, and the scenario's defined personality.
3.  **Objectives:** Try to achieve the specific learning objectives displayed on the screen (e.g., "Ask open-ended questions," "Empathize with the customer").

## Post-Session Assessment

Immediately after the session ends, you will receive an **Assessment Report**:

*   **Score:** A quantitative score (0-100) based on your performance.
*   **Feedback:** Specific, AI-generated feedback on what you did well and areas for improvement.
*   **Transcript:** A full text log of the conversation.
*   **Metrics:** Data on your speaking pace, listen-to-talk ratio, and sentiment.

## Technical Notes

*   **Latency:** The system aims for sub-300ms latency for natural conversation.
*   **Privacy:** Your voice data is processed in memory and redacted for PII (Personally Identifiable Information) before any long-term logging, ensuring compliance with privacy standards.
